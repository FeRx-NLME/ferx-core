- **`FitResult::total_ebe_nm_polished`** (#2571): under `inner_optimizer = nelder_mead`,
  counts the subjects whose Nelder–Mead stop was not a mode and had to be moved there by
  the gradient-based stationarity certificate. Those subjects are not fallbacks, so
  `total_ebe_fallbacks` no longer hides them inside an apparent Nelder–Mead success. The
  converged, non-fallback solves left over are the ones Nelder–Mead reached on its own. The
  count is always 0 under every other `inner_optimizer`, and saved fits written before this
  field load it as 0.
