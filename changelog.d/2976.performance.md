- **`optimizer = trust_region` is a Newton method on the exact Hessian** (#2975): every
  iteration forms the exact gradient and Hessian (the covariance step's R-matrix), solves the
  scaled, saddle-free trust-region subproblem exactly, and stops on the exact Newton decrement.
  It replaces the BHHH Hessian and truncated-CG subproblem, which on the same benchmark took
  2.5× the iterations of L-BFGS and ended above the best OFV on 65 of 130 fits; it now takes about a
  fifth of the iterations L-BFGS took before #2975, with none above. It now also accepts IOV models (the
  `E_OPTIMIZER_IOV` refusal is gone). `steihaug_max_iters` no longer has an effect.
