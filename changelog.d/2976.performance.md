- **`optimizer = trust_region` is PORT's model/trust-region method (`nlminb`'s) with exact
  second-order information** (#2975): exact gradient every iteration, a damped-BFGS model seeded
  with the saddle-free exact Hessian and refreshed from it, an exactly solved scaled subproblem, a
  stop certified on the exact Newton decrement, and the NLopt path's basin-shift restart. It
  replaces the BHHH Hessian and truncated-CG subproblem, which took 2.5× L-BFGS's iterations and
  ended above the best OFV on 65 of 130 benchmark fits. It now takes about 0.4× the iterations,
  with no fit above the best on 234 runs including starts off by 2× in θ and 10× in Ω and σ.
  It also accepts IOV models (the `E_OPTIMIZER_IOV` refusal is gone). `steihaug_max_iters` has
  no effect.
