- **Bayes reports when the conjugate mu-reference move stops working** (#1626).
  That move is Metropolis-corrected, so a rejected draw is a valid step and the
  posterior stays right — which also means a move rejected every sweep is
  invisible, while the thetas it owns quietly mix at random-walk speed. A fit
  now warns when it accepted fewer than half its draws, and names which outcome
  dominated: a draw outside a declared `theta_lower` / `theta_upper` box, a
  Metropolis rejection, or a conditional that could not be formed.
