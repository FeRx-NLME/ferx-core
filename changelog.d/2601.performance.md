- **Cold inner solves run the guarded multi-start once per subject, not twice** (#2601).
  A cold start under a seeded policy (#1504) solves twice, and both solves ran the twin
  seed and the per-coordinate scan. The seeded solve now skips them when its first descent
  ends at the unseeded solve's mode, where they would only repeat the same descents. On a
  synthetic 3,000-subject sparse two-compartment model with block Ω, a `maxiter = 0`
  evaluation drops from 0.57 s to 0.43 s at the same OFV. Subjects the multi-start does not
  move get the same EBEs as before. `FERX_PROFILE=1` now also reports how many multi-starts
  ran or were skipped, and how many subjects the twin seed, screen and scan moved.
