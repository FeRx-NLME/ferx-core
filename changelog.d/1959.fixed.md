- **A `TIME`-keyed level block keys its cells on the `TIME` as written** (#1959, #1796).
  An observation listed before a co-timed `MDV=1` or `EVID=2` row, or before a
  co-timed dose, was keyed on the time the reader moves it to for record ordering
  (e.g. `TIME=1.9999999999999998`), so `theta PLACEBO[STUDY, TIME]` grew a spurious
  cell next to `TIME=2` and the fit changed (on `mbma_placebo` with such rows: 30
  parameters instead of 24, objective 93.96 instead of 97.07). A stacked-reset
  subject's later periods are now keyed on their written `TIME` too, as NONMEM's
  `$PRED` sees it, and `E_THETA_LEVELS_DATA_MISMATCH` names one time, not two.
