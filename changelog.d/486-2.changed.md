- **IOV analytic gradients now cover up to 24 random effects per subject, down from 96** (#486).
  The adjoint serves any IOV subject whose stacked `[η, κ₁…κ_K]` count is at most 24, with no
  limit on θ. The forward provider it replaces served up to 96 stacked axes but capped the
  model at 24 `(θ, η, κ)`. A subject past 24 random effects takes the reconverged
  finite-difference gradient, and `FitResult.warnings` names it.
