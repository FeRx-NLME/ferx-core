- **`W_NONFINITE_OBJECTIVE` names the record whose own likelihood term is unusable**
  (#2266): for each subject it names, the warning now leads with the observations
  whose own `−2 log L` term (residual variance, `iiv_on_ruv` scale and M3 term
  included) is non-finite or past the `1e14` cutoff, with the `TIME`, `DV` and
  `IPRED` of the first. On a runaway EBE this is the `TIME 0` record a proportional
  error cannot score at a prediction of `0`, and both engines name it, where the
  first non-finite prediction pointed at a later record on the ODE and at none on
  the closed form. The structured `details` gain `unusable_terms` and
  `first_unusable_term_time`.
