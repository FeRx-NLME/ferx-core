- A basin-shift restart that reached the optimum was reported `converged: false` when its own
  step in θ was small, because it had not left its own start. Such a restart is now checked by
  restarting the optimizer from a perturbation of its point, as a warm start at an optimum is,
  and reported converged when that comes back to the same objective. On a simulated flip-flop
  population this costs about 110 more objective evaluations (#2707).
