- **Seeding a model from a fit now carries over its `[covariate_model]` coefficients**
  (#2864): `ModelEdit::SeedInits` (`ferx_update_model()`, a search's seeding) wrote
  estimates into `[parameters]` only, so a coefficient a `[covariate_model]` line
  generates restarted at the form's default and the seeded model was not the fit's
  optimum (19.85 OFV off on `examples/two_cpt_oral_covmodel.ferx` with bare lines). Each
  fitted coefficient is now written into its relation as `=> NAME(estimate)`, and a new
  `NAME(init)` form of the `=>` clause keeps the form's default bounds.
