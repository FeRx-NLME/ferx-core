- **Export a fitted DCM as a plain population PK model** (#1331): `ferx export-dcm
  run.fitrx` (or `nn::export::export_dcm_as_analytic`) writes every `[covariate_nn]`
  block out as ordinary `[individual_parameters]` equations at the fitted weights. The
  result loads without the `nn` feature and predicts exactly what the network does, bit
  for bit. `--fixed-theta` names each weight as a FIXed theta instead of inlining it.
