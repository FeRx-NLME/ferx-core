- **A gradient optimizer that stops without converging now says why** (#2713). After an
  L-BFGS, SLSQP, MMA or BFGS fit returns unconverged (budget left, not cancelled), ferx
  probes the reported estimates once: the objective with every EBE re-solved, along the
  fit's own outer gradient and along the exact one with every declined subject
  reconverged. A warning names the verdict: the fixed-EBE outer gradients stopped the line
  search (remedy: `reconverge_gradient_interval = 1`); the objective jumps where an EBE
  switches mode; the objective is not differentiable there (a kink); or a descent
  direction exists at that point, so the stop came from a trial point. Skipped under AGQ,
  for mixtures and for IOV models.
