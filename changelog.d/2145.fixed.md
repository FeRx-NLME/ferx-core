- **The covariance step no longer regularises or fails because of an identity-packed
  theta's units** (#2145). A theta with a negative lower bound is estimated as itself, so
  its row of `R` and `S` carries its units, and the eigenvalue floor could clip a
  positive-definite matrix when that theta was written in much larger or smaller units
  than the others. On the warfarin example with `V` written `1e5` to `1e6` times larger,
  `r` and `rsr` understated its standard error by 4.5× to 2000× and `s` failed outright.
  The floor is now measured with each free identity-packed theta's row rescaled to the
  other coordinates' scale. Models whose thetas are all log-packed are unaffected.
