- **The covariance step's finite-difference steps on a small θ packed as itself are now
  relative to its size** (#2147). A θ with a negative lower bound is packed as itself, and
  its step was `eps·(1 + |θ|)`, about `eps` in the θ's own units once `|θ| < 1`. On a θ of
  `0.0077` the finite-difference R matrix's step of about `0.01` crossed zero into the bound
  penalty, and every standard error collapsed (1000× too small on warfarin with `V` in
  litres ÷ 1000). The per-subject score that `covariance_method = s` and `rsr` build from
  stepped such a θ by 1–10 % of itself, a truncation error of up to 0.5 % in its SEs. Such a
  θ, when its declared initial value is non-zero and below 1 in magnitude, is now stepped by
  `eps·(|θ₀| + |θ|)`, as NONMEM scales a θ by its initial value. The SEs then match the same
  model written in units where `|θ| > 1` to about 1e-4. Every other parameter's step is unchanged
  bit for bit.
