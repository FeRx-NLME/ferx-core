- **Post-fit phases no longer stack their memory on top of estimation** (#3077): the compiled
  derivative plans a fit records are now released at each phase boundary — after estimation,
  around the covariance step, after the post-fit sensitivity sweep and around the ODE tolerance
  settle check — together with the pool workers' cached buffers. On a 9-state stiff ODE PK/PD
  model (150 subjects, 11 random effects) the peak memory footprint of a full fit fell from
  11.2 GB to 5.6 GB, with bit-identical estimates, objective and tables; later phases pay a few
  seconds to re-record their plans.
