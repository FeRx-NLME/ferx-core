- **SIR no longer floors, caps or rejects its draws because of an identity-packed theta's
  units** (#2179). A theta with a negative lower bound is sampled as itself, so its variance
  in the proposal carries its units, and the proposal's eigenvalue floor and bound caps
  could flatten every other direction when that theta was written in much larger or smaller
  units than the others. On the warfarin example with `V` written `1e5` times larger an
  SIR interval moved by 25 % and the ESS fell from 411 to 67; at `1e6` an interval moved
  26-fold, and at `1e8` every draw was rejected. The proposal is now floored and capped
  in the rescaled basis the covariance step uses, and so is the `covariance_fallback = sir`
  proposal built from a non-positive-definite Hessian. That basis also divides each Omega
  Cholesky off-diagonal by its row's estimated diagonal (#2274), so on a block-Omega model
  the SIR proposal, and with it the intervals and ESS, can change slightly even when no
  units change. Models with no identity-packed theta and no Omega block are unaffected.
