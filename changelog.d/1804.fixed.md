- **ODE models with more than 24 θ + η keep the analytic inner (EBE) gradient**
  (#1804). The 24-axis cap is now applied only to the outer gradient. The inner
  η-gradient is sized on the η count alone, so FIX covariate θ no longer push every
  subject's EBE search onto finite differences. On a model padded past the cap with
  zero-effect FIX θ, the FD inner had left a subject at a worse EBE and moved the
  objective by 16 OFV. At 25 axes the model now reproduces its 24-axis twin's
  objective exactly.
