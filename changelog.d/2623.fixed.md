- On binary- or Markov-only data, a `theta NAME[COL]` level block that no endpoint reads
  through its implicit index (one read as `NAME[COL]`, or not read at all) is no longer
  refused as "the data carries no observation rows". The `E_THETA_LEVEL_BINDING` refusal
  now says no endpoint reads the block through its implicit index and points at the
  expression; for an explicit `NAME[COL]` read it names the bare spelling `NAME` as the
  fix (#2623).
