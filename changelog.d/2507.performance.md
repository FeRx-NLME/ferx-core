- A FOCE/FOCEI NLopt fit no longer runs the covariance step on attempts it then
  discards. Every candidate attempt now runs without it: the held-cap retry, the
  basin-shift and mid-descent restarts, and the attempts they replace. The step
  runs once, on the attempt the fit reports, so a covariance fit that restarts no
  longer pays for two or more full covariance passes (#2507).
