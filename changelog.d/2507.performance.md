- A FOCE/FOCEI fit whose first leg ends on a basin-shift or mid-descent restart no
  longer runs the covariance step on that abandoned leg. The step now runs once, on
  the leg the fit reports — including when the restart is rejected or the restart
  chain is capped — so an `rsr`/`r` covariance fit that restarts no longer pays for
  two full covariance passes (#2507).
