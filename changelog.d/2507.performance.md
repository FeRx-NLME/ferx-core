- **FOCE / FOCEI L-BFGS fits spend less time after reaching their optimum** (#2507):
  a fit leg that is about to be restarted (a basin shift or a mid-descent stall) no
  longer runs a covariance step that the restart then discards. The step runs only
  for the result that is reported. A #2462 verification leg also now stops once
  `n + 1` evaluations pass without its best objective improving by more than 3e-6,
  instead of creeping on until its line search fails.
