- **`[fit_options] inner_kick = true` looks for a second EBE mode a kink puts a barrier away** (#3159): every inner solve restarts from a small kick of each coordinate and keeps a lower mode, which finds the second local minimum a clipped prediction can put up to 0.165 lower (not reachable by `inner_restarts`/`inner_screen`). Off by default, since it runs whether or not the model has a kink (measured 2-20× on four fast ODE examples without one).
