- SAEM's exploration phase now ends by NONMEM's termination test instead of a fixed
  150 iterations: it runs at least `n_exploration_min` (150) and at most
  `n_exploration_max` (2000) iterations, and stops at the first check where none of the
  complete-data −log L, θ, Ω and σ trajectories trends — each tested on its latest ten
  iterates spaced by its own autocorrelation time (clamped to 10–50; NONMEM `CTYPE=3
  CITER=10 CALPHA=0.01`, `CINTERVAL` chosen per coordinate). If the maximum is reached,
  the warning names the trajectories not yet shown stationary. Weakly identified
  variance components that were still decaying when the old fixed phase ended — and
  were then frozen by the averaging phase — now settle: on a public 5-η stand-in for
  `pk2_big` the importance-sampled −2 log L gap falls from +5.2 to +1.8 (median of 15
  seeds), at +60 % CPU; easier models stop after 300–700 iterations. **SAEM estimates
  change.** `n_exploration = n` still means a fixed length (and ignores the min/max).
  The length used is reported as `saem_n_exploration` in `-fit.yaml` /
  `FitResult::saem_n_exploration_used`. New option `smoothing_exponent` (default 1) sets
  the convergence-phase step `1/(k−K1)^a`. API: `FitOptions::saem_n_exploration` is now
  `Option<usize>` (`None` = automatic) (#2986).
