- SAEM's exploration phase now ends by a convergence test instead of a fixed 150
  iterations: it runs at least `n_exploration_min` (150) and at most
  `n_exploration_max` (500) iterations and stops at the first iteration where no
  Ω / σ / θ trajectory shows a significant trend (batch-means slope test, as in
  Monolix's and NONMEM's burn-in stops). Weakly identified variance components that
  were still decaying when the old fixed phase ended — and were then frozen by the
  averaging phase — now settle: on the 5-η `pk2_big` benchmark the importance-sampled
  −2 log L gap to the optimum falls from +10.6 to +2.9. Easy models stop at or just
  after 150. **SAEM estimates change.** `n_exploration = n` still means a fixed
  length (and ignores the min/max). The length used is reported as
  `saem_n_exploration` in `-fit.yaml` / `FitResult::saem_n_exploration_used`. New
  option `smoothing_exponent` (default 1) sets the convergence-phase step
  `1/(k−K1)^a`. API: `FitOptions::saem_n_exploration` is now `Option<usize>`
  (`None` = automatic) (#2986; number is a placeholder).
