- SAEM's schedule now follows NONMEM's `AUTO=1` (automatic exploration stop, 1000
  averaging iterations, two chains), and its E-step defaults to a gradient kernel (HMC on the
  AD engine's exact η-gradient). **SAEM estimates change.**
  - **Exploration** ends by NONMEM's termination test (`CTYPE=3 CITER=10 CALPHA=0.05`).
    Each tracked trajectory (complete-data −log L, θ, Ω, σ) is tested on its latest ten
    iterates spaced by its own autocorrelation time (clamped to 10–50; NONMEM picks
    `CINTERVAL` when it is 0). It runs between `n_exploration_min` (150) and
    `n_exploration_max` (4000, NONMEM's `NBURN` under `AUTO=1`). If the maximum is
    reached, the warning names the trajectories not yet shown stationary.
    `n_exploration = n` still means a fixed length.
  - **The averaging phase** (`n_convergence`) defaults to 1000 iterations (was 250;
    NONMEM's `NITER`). With step 1/k it removes what exploration left only slowly on
    weakly identified directions, and lengthening it improved every benchmark measured.
  - **NONMEM's η kernels** (`saem_kernel = nonmem`): per chain per iteration
    2 independence proposals from N(0, Ω) (M1), 2 from the chain's running conditional
    moments (M1B), 2 block random-walk proposals (M2) and 2 componentwise sweeps (M3),
    the random-walk scales adapted to `iaccept` 0.4 (`isample_m1`/`m1b`/`m2`/`m3`,
    `iaccept`). Same cost per iteration as the earlier `rw` kernels, but the exploration
    stop is met sooner (pk2_big stand-in 21–23 s against 24–42 s CPU) and
    `two_cpt_oral_cov` reaches its optimum on 10 of 20 seeds instead of 7.
  - **User-chosen E-step kernels.** Each kernel is a count per chain per iteration —
    `isample_hmc`, `isample_nuts` and `isample_mala` (new: gradient kernels), `isample_m1`,
    `isample_imh` (new: the f-SAEM Laplace proposal, during exploration only), `isample_m1b`,
    `isample_m2`, `isample_m3` — run in that order, any combination. `saem_kernel` is a preset that fills in the counts
    not set: `hmc` (**new default**) = `hmc 1, imh 1`, `nonmem` = `m1 2, m1b 2, m2 2, m3 2`, `nuts` =
    `nuts 1, imh 1`, `mala` = `mala 3, imh 1` (the jump runs as `m1` outside f-SAEM's scope);
    `rw` and `imh` keep their earlier kernels. The fit reports the schedule it ran
    (`saem_kernels` in `-fit.yaml`, `FitResult::saem_kernels`); all counts 0 is an error.
  - **Gradient kernels** `hmc` (preconditioned Hamiltonian Monte Carlo), `nuts` (Stan's
    No-U-Turn sampler) and `mala` (Metropolis-adjusted Langevin, one gradient per
    transition, its own step size adapted towards 0.574) on the AD engine's exact η-gradient, joint over η and κ under IOV,
    preconditioned by the chain's running moments. A chain the engine gives no gradient for
    runs `m2 2, m3 2` instead. Diagnostics in `FitResult::saem_gradient_kernel`. Against
    `nonmem`, `hmc` puts `two_cpt_oral_cov` at its optimum on 20 of 20 seeds (10), the
    pk2_big stand-in's mean gap at −0.09 (+0.20) and `warfarin_iov_saem`'s at +0.02
    (+0.19), in 23.8 against 27.3 s on pk2s.
    `mala` (`mala 3, imh 1`) ties `hmc` on all three models and runs pk2s in 21.9 against
    26.3 s. Under IOV the gradient kernels read each proposal's value from the gradient's AD
    pass (value/gradient fusion, as without IOV) instead of re-predicting the subject.
  - `mstep_optimizer = auto` runs Newton for SAEM's σ-only numerical M-step too (a combined
    error with every θ mu-referenced; was BOBYQA): Fisher scoring on the exact σ score and
    information, residual arithmetic on the memoized predictions. Same cost per iteration;
    on the pk2_big stand-in the exploration stop fires later (~10 % more CPU, same −2LL);
    `mstep_optimizer = bobyqa` restores the earlier solve.
  - Faster SAEM iterations: the post-M-step refresh scores each chain by the fused
    value/gradient pass and the gradient kernels run first from that gradient (−5 % per
    iteration on the pk2_big stand-in; the IOV refresh is now parallel too); the f-SAEM
    `imh` move runs during exploration only, as its authors used it (−6 to −9 % per
    iteration at the same −2LL); no per-observation allocation in the σ score
    (`ErrorModel::sigma_type`), and the IMH cost diagnostic times one call in 16.
  - The covariate mu-reference M-step solves on SA-averaged statistics (each chain-subject's
    η averaged and re-centred, SAEM's M-step on its sufficient statistics) instead of
    blending per-draw solutions, which averaged log-packed exponents geometrically (pk2_big
    stand-in `B_CL` +0.0024, 17/20 seeds at the optimum against 15/20). A mu that is affine
    in `(log θ, θ)` — power and exponential covariate models — takes one least-squares solve
    instead of Levenberg–Marquardt (the step fell from 8 % to 1.8 % of the fit).
  - A σ-only numerical M-step evaluates its score and information without the general path's
    per-θ rows and per-call allocations (bit-identical).
  - HMC integrates for exactly its jittered time T (`n = ⌈T/ε⌉` steps of `T/n`): ~7 % less
    CPU on the pk2_big stand-in at the same −2LL.
  - The covariate mu-reference M-step evaluates its typical values compiled to the VM,
    once per distinct covariate vector, with an exact Jacobian from the forward dual
    (was the tree-walking interpreter with central differences).
  - **Two η chains per subject** (`n_chains`, new, default 2; NONMEM's `ISAMPLE=2`).
    Implemented by replicating the subjects for the SAEM iterations only.
  - On a public 5-η stand-in for `pk2_big` the importance-sampled −2 log L gap falls from
    +4.63 (old 150/250 schedule) to +0.18 (mean of 10 seeds), and on `warfarin_iov_saem`
    from +0.82 to +0.14. The cost is about 3–5× the CPU per fit (pk2s 6.1 → 28.9 s).
    `two_cpt_oral_cov` ends in a V1–KA trap on about half the seeds under either schedule.
  - The SAEM chain copies are registered with the fit's kernel-JIT scope, so a two-chain
    run does not re-fingerprint subjects per likelihood evaluation (−36 % per iteration).
  - SAEMOBJ (`saem_ofv = saem`) pools the chains per subject, as NONMEM averages its
    `ISAMPLE` samples.
  - The length used is reported as `saem_n_exploration` in `-fit.yaml` /
    `FitResult::saem_n_exploration_used`.
  - New option `smoothing_exponent` (default 1) sets the convergence-phase step
    `1/(k−K1)^a`.
  - API: `FitOptions::saem_n_exploration` is now `Option<usize>` (`None` = automatic);
    new `FitOptions::saem_n_chains`; the `saem_isample_*` counts are `Option<usize>`
    (`None` = the preset's), with new `saem_isample_imh`, `saem_isample_hmc` and
    `saem_isample_nuts`, `saem_isample_mala`; new `SaemKernel::Hmc` / `Nuts` / `Mala`, `FitResult::saem_kernels` and
    `FitResult::saem_gradient_kernel` (#2986).
