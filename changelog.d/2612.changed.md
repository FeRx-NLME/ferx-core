- **SAEM's default M-step is now SAEM's** (#2612): a theta with no ETA and a combined-error
  sigma move by stochastic approximation on the score (`mstep_solver = score_sa`), as NONMEM
  moves them, instead of the single-draw derivative-free re-maximisation whose average
  carries a Jensen bias (#1458). Outside `score_sa`'s scope (mixtures, M3, TTE,
  `block_sigma`, a `[covariate_nn]` theta, a residual magnitude) the old rule remains, with a
  warning. SAEM estimates move: on a five-random-effect benchmark the importance-sampled
  −2 log L improves from −3501.2 ± 3.3 to −3505.7 ± 1.2 over nine seeds. `mstep_solver =
  bobyqa; isample_m1 = 0; isample_m1b = 0` restores the previous behaviour.
