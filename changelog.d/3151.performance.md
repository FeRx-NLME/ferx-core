- **A power with an estimated exponent below 1 at a zero base in `[odes]` keeps the exact outer gradient** (#3151):
  `(central/V)^GAMMA` read `0^(GAMMA−1) = ∞` from a state that is 0 at the dose time, and the
  reverse pass turned it into `NaN`, so every subject fell back to finite-difference gradients
  (about 11× slower on the reproduction) once `GAMMA` drifted below 1.
