- **The gradient optimizers scale every coordinate by its curvature** (#2975): under
  `parameter_scaling = auto`, `nlopt_lbfgs` and `slsqp` divide each packed coordinate by
  `1/√|∂²OFV/∂x²|` at the start (the exact R-matrix diagonal), replacing the magnitude rules.
  On 26 FOCEI benchmark models from five starts each: 24% fewer iterations and 25% fewer inner
  solves for the typical model, every fit at the same optimum; 17% less wall time on the
  wall-time set. Three models with a variance started far from its estimate are slower
  (documented in `docs/estimation/parameterization.qmd`). Verbose output reports the scales.
