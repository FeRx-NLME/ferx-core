- **The gradient optimizers scale every coordinate by its curvature** (#2975): under
  `parameter_scaling = auto`, `nlopt_lbfgs` and `slsqp` now divide each packed coordinate by
  `1/√|∂²OFV/∂x²|` at the start (exact, or by finite differences without exact derivatives),
  replacing the magnitude rules. On 29 FOCEI benchmark fits: 23% fewer iterations and 31%
  fewer inner solves in total, every fit at the same optimum (e.g. `three_cpt_iv` 62 → 29
  iterations, `two_cpt_oral_cov` 53 → 25). Verbose output reports the scales.
