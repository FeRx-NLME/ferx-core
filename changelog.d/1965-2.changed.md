- **IMPMAP rechecks each subject's MAP from a cold start** (#1965; not yet for `[mixture]`
  models): new fit option
  `impmap_map_recheck` (default `10`). Every 10 iterations, and always for the final EBEs,
  each subject is also solved from a cold start (η = 0; FREM covariate ETAs at
  their data-implied values) and the better mode is kept, so a subject stuck
  in a local mode (flip-flop absorption, a weakly identified KA) no longer biases the
  estimates. On `two_cpt_oral_cov` this took the spread of the −2LL across seeds from 1.8
  to 0.001, at 0–12% extra wall time. Seeded IMPMAP results can change; set
  `impmap_map_recheck = 0` for the previous behaviour.
