- **`method = gn` now warns when the data have fewer subjects than free parameters**
  (#2438). Gauss–Newton's BHHH information is one rank-one term per subject, so with fewer
  subjects than parameters it is singular by construction, and its steps can stall short of
  the optimum. On the joint PK + TTE example (6 subjects, 7 free parameters) it stopped
  6 OFV units above the optimum, reported as not converged. The warning says why and points
  to `method = gn_hybrid`, which reaches the optimum there.
