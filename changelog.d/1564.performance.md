- **L-BFGS / SLSQP fits stop sooner when their stop is already the optimum** (#1564). The
  #2462 verification leg (5 to 20 objective evaluations at `inner_tol = 1e-10`) is skipped
  when an exact certificate shows nothing is left: the Newton decrement of the exact
  Hessian, at the modes re-solved at the leg's tolerance, is at most 1e-6. The certificate
  is exact or absent, never approximated; a stop it cannot certify runs the legs as before.
  With `covariance = true` its per-subject Hessian terms are handed to the covariance step,
  which reuses them bit for bit, and a restarted NLopt fit now runs one covariance step
  instead of one per attempt. Measured on #1564's benchmark kit with the covariance step
  (one core, interleaved A/B, every OFV identical): 1-cpt oral 1.53× faster, 2-cpt oral
  1.16×, their ODE twins 1.77× and 1.55×; the Michaelis–Menten benchmark, which is not
  certified, runs 1.07× faster from its single covariance step. See
  [Optimizers](https://ferx-nlme.github.io/ferx-core/estimation/optimizers.html#gradient-stop-certificate).
