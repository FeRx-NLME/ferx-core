- **An ETA or kappa that reaches one scale twice no longer prints a CV% or SD**
  (#1724): `TVV * exp(ETA_V) * exp(ETA_V)`, `exp(ETA_V + ETA_V)`, an
  intermediate read twice, a diamond of intermediates, or a reassignment that
  adds the ETA again enters its scale as `2·η`, whose CV is `√(e^{4ω} − 1)`
  (70.1% at ω = 0.1), not the `100·√ω` (31.6%) that was printed. These rows are
  now labelled `custom`. The two branches of an `if` (and `min`/`max`) still
  count once.
