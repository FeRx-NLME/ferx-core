- **The finite-difference covariance matrices of an ODE model now integrate at
  `ode_reltol = 1e-6` / `ode_abstol = 1e-8`** when the fit itself ran looser (#520).
  The R-matrix stencil divides by `h²` and so amplified the integration noise of the
  default `1e-4` / `1e-6`: on a 3-compartment IV ODE model the standard errors came out
  2–7× too large with no warning, and once an eigenvalue was floored, 4400× too large.
  The score matrix `S` of `covariance_method = s` / `rsr`, whose per-subject scores are
  finite-differenced too, runs at the same tolerance. The fit, its estimates and its OFV
  are unchanged, and the exact analytic R-matrix most Gaussian `[odes]` fits take keeps
  the fit's tolerance. A fit pinned tighter keeps its own; closed-form transit /
  inverse-Gaussian models are covered through their absorption ODE twin. If the tighter
  run cannot be set up, the step runs at the fit's tolerance and says so
  (`W_COV_ODE_TOLERANCE_NOT_TIGHTENED`). Measured cost: 0.95–1.17× the stencil's wall
  time. **Results move**: standard errors at the default tolerance of ODE models on the
  finite-difference R route — `analytic_cov_hessian = false`, `gradient = fd`, mixtures,
  non-Gaussian endpoints, models outside the sensitivity scope, and subjects salvaged
  per subject — and, under `covariance_method = s` or `rsr`, of every ODE model.
