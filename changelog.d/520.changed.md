- **The finite-difference covariance step of an ODE model now integrates at
  `ode_reltol = 1e-6` / `ode_abstol = 1e-8`** when the fit itself ran looser (#520).
  The stencil divides by `h²` and so amplified the integration noise of the default
  `1e-4` / `1e-6`: on a 3-compartment IV ODE model the standard errors came out 2–7×
  too large with no warning, and once an eigenvalue was floored, 4400× too large. Only
  the stencil is tightened — the fit, its estimates and its OFV are unchanged, and the
  exact analytic R-matrix most Gaussian `[odes]` fits take keeps the fit's tolerance.
  A fit pinned tighter keeps its own. Measured cost: 0.95–1.17× the stencil's wall time.
  **Results move**: standard errors of ODE models (and of closed-form transit /
  inverse-Gaussian models whose subjects reach the absorption ODE twin) on the
  finite-difference covariance route — `analytic_cov_hessian = false`, `gradient = fd`,
  mixtures, non-Gaussian endpoints, models outside the sensitivity scope, and subjects
  salvaged per subject for having too many random effects — at the default tolerance.
