- **Adaptive-dosing refusals now carry a code once the driver runs** (#1983): a
  controller action the driver refuses (a dose into compartment 0, past the model's
  states, into an input-rate or lagged compartment, a bad amount or rate, or a `Stop`
  with actions after it) is `E_ADAPTIVE_CONTROLLER_ACTION`; a decision time that
  collides with a subject's own times is `E_ADAPTIVE_OPTIONS`; a base dose the driver
  cannot replay, a non-finite break time, or a `TAD` read before any dose is
  `E_ADAPTIVE_UNSUPPORTED`. `max_decisions`, a monitor compartment out of range, a `Dv`
  monitor with no `[error_model]` (`E_ADAPTIVE_SPEC` on `simulate_adaptive_from_spec`),
  and a base regimen with an EVID=3/4 reset under a time-varying covariate or IOV are now
  refused before any subject is simulated, so those messages no longer start with
  `subject '…' (sim N):` (the reset one starts with `subject '…':`). A frozen-replay or
  snapshot verification failure still carries no code.
