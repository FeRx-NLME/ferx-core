- **A trough listed before a co-timed dose at the first instant reads pre-dose on ODE
  models** (#2124): an observation written before a dose at the same time is read one
  ULP earlier, but when that was the subject's first instant (at a time of at most 8)
  every ODE engine applied the dose first, so the trough read post-dose. This hit
  `predict()`, `fit()` (through its gradient's walk too, so the objective scored the
  wrong value), the sdtab state columns, `[derived]`, the survival hazard pass and
  `simulate_adaptive`. NONMEM reads the observation before the dose, and now so does
  ferx; troughs at later doses were already right.
