- **`saem_accumulation = polyak`**: an opt-in SAEM accumulation phase that runs one
  stochastic-approximation recursion on the marginal score for every parameter, with
  Polyak–Ruppert averaging and a zero-variance control variate built from the exact
  η-gradient (`saem_control_variate`)
  ([#2721](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2721)). On #1445's sparse
  combined-error design it lowers the importance-sampled −2 log L by 1.5 units on average
  over 10 seeds, with less seed-to-seed spread.
