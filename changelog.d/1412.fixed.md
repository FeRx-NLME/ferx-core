- **The ODE event-time search now counts its steps in the solver diagnostics** (#1412). The
  cumulative-hazard threshold search behind `simulate()`'s TTE draws and the adaptive driver
  ran on its own driver, which never reported to the solver-statistics scope. So its
  attempted, accepted and rejected steps, its min-`dt` clamps and its stiff escalations read
  `0` behind `W_ODE_SOLVER_DIAGNOSTICS` even on a search that integrated. They are counted
  now. A stiff attempt the search throws away and re-solves explicitly is counted as a
  rejected escalation, with its clamps marked discarded, as on the other solves. The segment
  whose search fails counts nothing: `W_TTE_SOLVE_FAILED` already reports it, and the solver advice
  would not fit a right-hand side that diverged. Locating the crossing inside the bracketing
  step only interpolates, so it adds no steps.
