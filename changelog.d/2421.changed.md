- **Every `theta NAME[COLS]` level block is rank-checked at bind time** (#2421):
  a block whose coded levels move `y` exactly as other θ do is refused under the
  contrast it resolves to, naming those θ, whether or not it absorbs a random
  effect. Before, only a block rescuing a random effect was checked, so
  `TVE0 + PLACEBO` under `contrast = none` bound with a flat direction. `auto`
  moves a block that absorbs nothing to the next contrast that keeps its rank, a
  nested block `auto` resolves to `sum_to_zero_within` is checked too, and a
  within-group contrast whose remaining direction the other θ carry is refused
  naming the random effect. Models that bound aliased before are now refused.
