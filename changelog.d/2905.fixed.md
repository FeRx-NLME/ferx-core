- A refit started at a previous fit's optimum, on a model whose individual objectives have
  more than one mode, could come back with `W_STALLED_AT_INIT` (and, on some, `converged:
  false`): the check that the start is an optimum restarted from a perturbation of it, the
  restart's EBEs landed in another mode, and the check gave no verdict. That restart is now
  resumed as a fit's own leg is before it is judged, so such a refit is verified as a warm
  start. On a simulated flip-flop population this costs about 75 more objective evaluations
  (#2905).
