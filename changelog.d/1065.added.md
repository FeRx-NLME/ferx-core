- **A θ level block can be declared `profile`** (#1065):
  `theta PLACEBO[STUDY, TIME](0.0) profile` marks a block whose levels will be solved
  for at every outer step instead of searched by the optimizer. This release accepts
  and checks the declaration only — a fit of a profiled block is refused with
  `E_PROFILE_SCOPE` until the profile solve lands. `ferx check` already reports what
  a profiled block cannot combine with (`E_PROFILE_SCOPE`: a method other than
  `foce`/`focei`, a non-additive residual error, a non-Gaussian endpoint, bounds or
  `FIX` on the block, …) and a prediction that does not read it linearly with a
  data-only coefficient (`E_PROFILE_NOT_LINEAR`). `FitResult.profiled_blocks` and the
  fit YAML's `profiled_blocks` / `theta_blocks.<name>.profiled` name the profiled
  blocks, and their levels count in `n_parameters` and BIC.
