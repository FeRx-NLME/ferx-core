- **Runs under consecutive seeds no longer share random streams** (#3010): per-subject,
  per-chain and per-start RNG streams (IMP, IMPMAP, SAEM, SAEM conditional distribution,
  Bayes chains, NPDE, multi-start) were seeded `seed + i`, so seed `s` subject `i` and seed
  `s + 1` subject `i - 1` drew identically and a 5-seed IMP comparison shared 499 of 500
  subject streams. They are now a 64-bit hash of `(seed, coordinates)`. Results at a fixed
  seed are still reproducible but differ from earlier versions.
