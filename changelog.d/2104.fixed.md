- **A non-positive or non-finite `CL` or `V` gets the same prediction on both closed-form
  engines** (#2104). A subject with a time-varying covariate is served by the event-driven
  walk, which used to skip the elimination step when `CL` or `V` was `≤ 0`, so the dose stayed
  in the body: a `CL ≤ 0` on an IV model served `AMT / V` from the dose on, where a subject
  with constant covariates (superposition) was served 0. Both engines now drop such a dose and
  serve 0, `+inf` included (it used to give `NaN` on one engine and 0 on the other). A `NaN`
  `CL` or `V` now gives `NaN` on both from the first dose's arrival on — including an
  observation exactly at a bolus or the start of an infusion — and 0 before any dose. The
  analytic gradients follow the same rule. A fit whose final estimates put a non-positive or
  non-finite `CL` or `V` on any record now warns (`W_DEGENERATE_CL_V`, code
  `degenerate_clearance_or_volume`), since NONMEM would reject that record rather than predict.
