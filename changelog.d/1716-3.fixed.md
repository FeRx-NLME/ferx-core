- **Bayes mixes when the conjugate θ move is off**
  ([#1716](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/1716)): a log mu-referenced typical
  value left to the random walk (a model with a non-Gaussian random effect, or with only some
  ETAs mu-referenced) is now also moved jointly with its ETAs (`log θ + δ`, `η − δ`), which
  leaves every individual parameter unchanged. On warfarin with a Gamma clearance the chains
  went from ESS ≈ 3 and R̂ 2.4–3.3 to ESS 4,000–28,000 and R̂ ≤ 1.001, and agree with Stan.
