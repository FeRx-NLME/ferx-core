- **FOCE/FOCEI fits skip a verification leg that has nothing to gain** (#2462): the
  tight-tolerance leg that checks a gradient optimizer's stop now ends at its first point
  when a Newton step from there is predicted (BHHH decrement) to gain at most 1e-7 OFV.
  Such legs used to spend 5 to 22 evaluations at `inner_tol = 1e-10` to gain at most
  1.5e-8. Fits are 16–28% faster on `transit_2cpt` and `warfarin_ode_lagtime` and 3–5%
  faster on `warfarin`, `two_cpt_oral_cov` and `three_cpt_iv`; the objective is
  unchanged to six decimals at the default `inner_tol`. A leg predicted to gain more
  runs as before.
