- **Every copula Copulas.jl 1.0.1 has that can be a random-effect prior**
  ([#1716](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/1716)): a `copula (ETA_A, ETA_B) ~
  FAMILY(θ)` line joins ETAs declared on their own `omega` lines, each keeping its own margin,
  with the Archimedean families (Clayton, Frank, Gumbel, Joe, AMH, inverse Gaussian,
  Gumbel–Barnett, BB1–BB3, BB6–BB10), the extreme-value ones (Galambos, mixed, Hüsler–Reiss,
  extremal-t, asymmetric logistic / Galambos / mixed), the Archimax BB4 and BB5, FGM and
  Plackett, and the constructions `survival(…)`, `rotated90/180/270(…)`, `khoudraji(…)`,
  `liebscher(…)` and `nested(…)`. The t copula is `family = t(NU)` on a `block_omega`
  copula. Each is checked against Copulas.jl, with exact gradients under every estimator.
