- `optimizer = auto` now resolves to `trust_region` whenever the exact FOCE/FOCEI gradient is available (it resolved to `nlopt_lbfgs`). On a 26-model benchmark from 9 starts each, the trust region reached the best OFV on all 234 fits, where L-BFGS stopped above it three times while reporting convergence, in 0.41× the iterations and the fewest CPU cycles. Finite-difference and mixture fits still take `bobyqa`, `laplace` keeps `nlopt_lbfgs`, and `optimizer = nlopt_lbfgs` restores the previous behaviour (#2976).
- `optimizer = trust_region` now follows the other outer optimizers' safety rules (#2976). It refuses trial points the EBE convergence guard rejects (`max_unconverged_frac`), and a start with no feasible objective stops not converged with `W_NO_FEASIBLE_EVAL`. It writes checkpoints, falls back to a finite-difference model on IOV models outside the exact scope, records whether the fit left its start (`left_init`), and sizes its first step from the exact model's Newton step.
- `optimizer = trust_region` now accepts the quadrature objectives (`laplace`, `focei` with `n_agq > 1`), whose exact gradient and Hessian it differentiates; the `E_OPTIMIZER_AGQ` refusal is gone (#2976).
