- `optimizer = auto` now resolves to `trust_region` whenever the exact FOCE/FOCEI gradient and Hessian are available (it resolved to `nlopt_lbfgs`, which it still does with the exact gradient alone). On a 26-model benchmark from 9 starts each, the trust region reached the best OFV on all 234 fits, where L-BFGS stopped above it three times while reporting convergence, in 0.41× the iterations and the fewest CPU cycles. Finite-difference and mixture fits still take `bobyqa`, `laplace` keeps `nlopt_lbfgs`, and `optimizer = nlopt_lbfgs` restores the previous behaviour (#2976).
