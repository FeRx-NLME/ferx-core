- **FOCE fits with many observations per subject are fast again past the Woodbury gate**
  (#2988). Where a row's residual variance sits on its floor (`1 + tr S > 1e6`), the
  outer gradient factored the subject's full observation-sized `R̃`, which the compiled
  per-subject plans write out as `O(n³)` instructions. It now rotates each row into the
  factor of `I + S` instead (`O(n)` in the observations, no cancellation). On an IOV
  model with 100 and 200 one-hour infusions per subject, a fit takes 5.4 s and 11.5 s
  instead of 29 s and 396 s (7.0 s and 22 s before #2939; macOS arm64, `--threads 1`),
  with the same OFV.
