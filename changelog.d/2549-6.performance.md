- **Laplace and AGQ fits take predicted EBE warm starts** (#2549): the inner solves of
  `method = laplace` and FOCEI with `n_agq > 1` now start at the modes predicted for the new
  point, `η̂ + (dη̂/dx)·Δx`, with the exact mode response formed on the quadrature's own
  gradient tape (the conditional mode's, from the exact inner Hessian). On a hard fit it
  decides the result: Laplace on a 26-parameter, five-random-effect model reaches OFV
  −3485.11 in 59 G instructions where it stopped at −3458.81 after 90 G. The exact inner
  Hessian is formed once per subject and evaluation: the quadrature objective's exact anchor
  and the next warm inner solve's seed both take it from the gradient pass.
