- **`optimizer = auto` runs L-BFGS on a `[mixture]` model** (#2739), like any other
  model in the analytic gradient's scope, instead of always BOBYQA. On the public mixture
  fixtures it reaches the best optimum about as often from random starts (within 3 of 100
  fits), in 5–8× fewer evaluations. A mixture with two classes that start indistinguishable
  (every subject has the same mixing probability and the same objective under both) still
  runs BOBYQA, since a gradient optimizer cannot separate them; the fit warns
  `W_MIXTURE_SYMMETRIC_START`, naming the pair, and reports `auto (bobyqa)` with
  `gradient_method_outer` `N/A`. The check is exact, so classes that start only close to
  each other run L-BFGS without a warning. A subject outside the analytic gradient's
  scope has its terms finite-differenced on its own (#2906), which can still make `auto`
  slower than BOBYQA there. Pin `optimizer = bobyqa` to keep the old behaviour.
