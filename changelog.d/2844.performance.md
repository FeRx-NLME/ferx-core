- **`sqrt` in an `[odes]` right-hand side now compiles** (#2844): it runs as register and
  native code instead of keeping the whole block on the bytecode interpreter. Predictions,
  inner gradients and inner Hessians are bit-identical to the interpreter's; the outer
  gradient's compiled reverse sweep agrees with it to rounding, as for every other compiled
  function. A quasi-steady-state TMDD model written with the `sqrt` form of the free-drug
  quadratic fits 3.3x faster (13.9 s to 4.3 s on one Linux x86_64 thread, same OFV), and
  `W_ODE_RHS_INTERPRETED` no longer names `sqrt`. `sqrt` of a negative zero now keeps its sign
  on every platform.
