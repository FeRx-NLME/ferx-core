- **`sqrt` in an `[odes]` right-hand side now compiles** (#2844): it runs as register and
  native code, bit-identical to the interpreter, instead of keeping the whole block on the
  bytecode interpreter. A quasi-steady-state TMDD model written with the `sqrt` form of the
  free-drug quadratic fits 2.4x faster (24.4 s to 10.3 s on one Mac thread, same OFV), and
  `W_ODE_RHS_INTERPRETED` no longer names `sqrt`. The scalar `f64::guard_floor` now keeps the
  sign of a zero argument, as the dual types always did.
