- **SIR weights every draw with the objective the fit minimised** (#1755): a Laplace
  fit's `run_sir` and `run_covariance` now take `method` from the fit instead
  of the caller's options (default FOCEI), and the in-fit SIR of a method chain such as
  `[focei, laplace]` scores the final estimating stage rather than the top-level
  `method`. A `[mixture]` model's draws are scored with the K-class mixture marginal the
  fit reports, not the one-class objective, which on a class-override fit had left an
  SIR effective sample size of about 1 and intervals excluding the estimates.
  `run_sir(fit)` now equals the in-fit SIR at the same settings; `n_agq` is still read
  from the caller's options (#1758).
