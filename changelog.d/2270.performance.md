- The inner EBE solver now converges from a start next to the mode instead of falling back to
  Nelder–Mead. A curvature pair from a short step updates the BFGS metric, and on an exact
  objective a step whose gain is below the objective's rounding is verified by the gradient.
  Since #2261 every IOV warm start is the previous mode, so this is the common case: on
  `nested_levels_3` the inner objective evaluations fall from 100,960 to 16,879, and non-IOV fits
  gain too (`examples/warfarin` 143,927 → 18,424). (#2270)
