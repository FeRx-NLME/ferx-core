- **An ODE model with IOV no longer stalls at its initial estimate** (#2212): since
  #2202 the first outer evaluation starts each subject's inner solve from the
  pre-flight EBEs, and on an ODE+IOV model whose initial estimate sat far from the
  data every one of those starts was rejected as degenerate. The fit returned its
  starting OFV with `W_NO_FEASIBLE_EVAL`. Starts known to be the mode are no
  longer rejected.
