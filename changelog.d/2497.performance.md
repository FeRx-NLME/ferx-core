- **Tight-tolerance inner solves no longer stall at the mode** (#2497): at a tight
  `inner_tol`, which is what the #2462 verification leg uses, an inner BFGS that has reached
  its mode now stops after five steps that move neither the objective, the iterate nor the
  gradient, instead of repeating them to `inner_maxiter`. When the Newton certificate shows
  that point is at its mode, the solve skips the Nelder–Mead restart. This applies to IOV and
  non-IOV subjects alike, but not to FREM. On the bundled examples the restart returns the
  same NLL and EBEs, bit for bit. Measured on macOS arm64 under load, with the same OFV to
  the six reported decimals: a one-compartment IIV+IOV FOCEI fit drops from 25.9 s to 2.0 s,
  and a 3-compartment IV FOCEI fit from 3.0 s to 1.0 s.
