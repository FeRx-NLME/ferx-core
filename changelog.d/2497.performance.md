- **Tight-tolerance inner solves no longer stall at the mode** (#2497): at a tight
  `inner_tol`, which is what the #2462 verification leg uses, an inner BFGS that has reached
  its mode now stops after five steps that move neither the objective nor its gradient,
  instead of repeating them to `inner_maxiter`. When the Newton certificate shows that point
  is at its mode, the solve counts as converged and skips the Nelder–Mead restart. This
  applies to IOV and non-IOV subjects alike. On the bundled examples the restart returns the
  same NLL and EBEs, bit for bit. Measured on macOS arm64: a one-compartment IIV+IOV FOCEI fit drops
  from 18.7 s to 1–2.5 s at the same OFV, and a 3-compartment IV FOCEI fit from 2.6–3.8 s
  to about 1.2 s.
