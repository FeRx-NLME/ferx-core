- **Tight-tolerance inner solves no longer stall at the mode** (#2497): at a tight
  `inner_tol`, which is what the #2462 verification leg uses, an inner BFGS that has reached
  its mode now stops after five steps that leave the objective unchanged instead of repeating
  them to `inner_maxiter`. An IOV subject that stopped at its mode (Newton certificate)
  counts as converged and skips the Nelder–Mead restart. Same optimum (OFV within 3e-6). Measured
  on macOS arm64, a one-compartment IIV+IOV FOCEI fit goes from 18.7 s to 1.3 s and a
  3-compartment IV FOCEI fit from 2.6 s to 1.1 s.
