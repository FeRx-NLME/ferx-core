- **FREM models: Gauss–Newton gradients and the `s` / `rsr` covariance score now
  use the covariate σ on covariate rows** (#2023). The closed-form per-subject
  gradient read the variance of each covariate pseudo-observation off the PK error
  model. The covariate σ got no score and the PK σ got a spurious one. Two paths
  used that gradient. Under `method = gn` / `gn_hybrid` it and its BHHH Hessian
  disagreed with the objective the line search evaluates. Under
  `covariance_method = s` or `rsr` it fed the score matrix of a FOCE or FOCEI fit.
  FREM subjects now take the finite-difference gradient of the objective.
