- **A model with no random effects fits faster** (#2763). Its event-model objective is now
  evaluated on plain numbers instead of through an empty sensitivity jet, which gives the same
  value, and it skips the L-BFGS / SLSQP verification leg, which exists to tighten an inner
  solve such a model does not have. On `nonmem_anchor/pktte_tdep_fit.ferx` with its three
  hazard parameters estimated (`TVGAM` bounded to (0.001, 10)), the fit takes 24 iterations
  instead of 36 (0.80 s instead of 1.43 s) at the same OFV, and on that dataset
  replicated to 240 subjects 4.84 s instead of 6.28 s. With the bounds in the issue (`TVGAM`
  in (−5, 5), `TVH0` down to 1e-5) it takes 22 iterations instead of 26 (0.99 s instead of
  1.13 s). Linux x86_64, one thread.
