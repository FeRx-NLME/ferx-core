- **Non-Gaussian random-effect distributions on the omega line**
  ([#1716](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/1716)): `omega ETA ~ ω density KIND`,
  with every distribution Pumas's `@random` block takes for a scalar or vector random effect
  (t, logistic, Laplace, Cauchy, Gumbel, NIG, p-generalized Gaussian, GEV, the bounded kernels,
  gamma, inverse gamma, log-normal (`lognormal`, median 1: the classic `TV·exp(η)`, compiled
  as that Gaussian ETA; `lognormal_mean`, mean 1), chi, Weibull, Fréchet, inverse Gaussian, beta prime,
  noncentral χ², Pareto, GPD, Lévy, beta, logit-normal and more) and `truncated(KIND, lo, hi)`.
  As in Pumas, the ETA is the distributed quantity: `CL = TVCL * ETA_CL` under `density gamma`
  is a Gamma clearance of mean `TVCL`. Blocks take `density t(ν)` (multivariate t),
  `density lognormal` / `lognormal_mean` (multivariate log-normal) and `density copula(ETA_A = KIND, …)` (a
  Gaussian copula of named univariate margins); IOV `kappa` lines take a density; FREM and
  `[mixture]` class overrides work with density ETAs; Bayes takes
  `bayes_omega_prior = inversewishart | wishart | lkj(η)`. The exact prior enters FOCE, FOCEI,
  Laplace, IMP/IMPMAP, SAEM/MCEM, Bayes and VI with exact gradients, and simulation draws each
  distribution. Kinds with a kink or a positive-density edge are refused where the reported
  objective reads the prior's curvature (the FOCE family, Laplace, `vi_final_ofv = laplace`,
  `saem_ofv = focei`); under IMP their `ofv`, AIC and BIC are the importance-sampling −2 log L,
  and the covariance step is skipped (`W_DENSITY_NO_COVARIANCE`). A positive density ETA read
  under `exp`/`log` is refused with the line to write, and a density with a shape transform on
  the same ETA is refused. A density ETA's omega or KAPPA row is followed by its distribution,
  its exact mean with CV% or SD, and the 5th/50th/95th percentiles of its effect with 95%
  intervals from the covariance matrix (console and fit YAML). EBEs, the conditional
  distribution and supplied η (`SuppliedEtas`) are on the effect's scale, and shrinkage is
  computed there (by IQR where the effect has no closed-form moments). Every θ row that is a
  parameter's typical value says whether it is the mean, median or geometric mean
  (`typical_value_of` / `typical_value_statistic` in the fit YAML). A shape transform is declared in
  `[eta_shape]` only, and an `[eta_shape]` `tdist` θ whose bounds reach ν < 3 is refused
  ([#1731](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/1731)).
