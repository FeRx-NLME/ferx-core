- **Non-Gaussian random-effect distributions on the omega line**
  ([#1716](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/1716)): `omega ETA ~ ω density KIND`,
  with every distribution Pumas's `@random` block takes for a scalar or vector random effect
  (t, logistic, Laplace, Cauchy, Gumbel, NIG, p-generalized Gaussian, GEV, the bounded kernels,
  gamma, inverse gamma, log-normal, chi, Weibull, Fréchet, inverse Gaussian, beta prime,
  noncentral χ², Pareto, GPD, Lévy, beta, logit-normal and more) and `truncated(KIND, lo, hi)`.
  As in Pumas, the ETA is the distributed quantity: `CL = TVCL * ETA_CL` under `density gamma`
  is a Gamma clearance of mean `TVCL`. Blocks take `density t(ν)` (multivariate t),
  `density lognormal` (multivariate log-normal) and `density copula(ETA_A = KIND, …)` (a
  Gaussian copula of named univariate margins); IOV `kappa` lines take a density; FREM and
  `[mixture]` class overrides work with density ETAs; Bayes takes
  `bayes_omega_prior = inversewishart | wishart | lkj(η)`. The exact prior enters FOCE, FOCEI,
  Laplace, IMP/IMPMAP, SAEM/MCEM, Bayes and VI with exact gradients, and simulation draws each
  distribution; kinds with a kink or a positive-density edge are refused under the FOCE family
  and Laplace, which read the prior's curvature. A density ETA's omega or KAPPA row reports its
  distribution, its exact mean with CV% or SD, and the 5th/50th/95th percentiles of its effect
  (console and fit YAML); its shrinkage is computed on the effect's scale, and
  `io::output::eta_effects` gives the EBEs on that scale. A shape transform is declared in
  `[eta_shape]` only, and an `[eta_shape]` `tdist` θ whose bounds reach ν < 3 is refused
  ([#1731](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/1731)).
