- **Non-Gaussian random-effect distributions on the omega line**
  ([#1716](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/1716)): `omega ETA ~ ω density KIND`,
  with every distribution Pumas's `@random` block takes for a scalar or vector random effect
  (t, logistic, Laplace, Cauchy, Gumbel, NIG, p-generalized Gaussian, GEV, the bounded kernels,
  gamma, inverse gamma, log-normal, chi, Weibull, Fréchet, inverse Gaussian, beta prime,
  noncentral χ², Pareto, GPD, Lévy, beta, logit-normal and more) and `truncated(KIND, lo, hi)`;
  a `t(ν)` `block_omega` is multivariate t. As in Pumas, the ETA is the
  distributed quantity: `CL = TVCL * ETA_CL` under `density gamma` is a Gamma clearance of mean
  `TVCL`. The exact prior enters FOCE/FOCEI/Laplace, IMP/IMPMAP, SAEM/MCEM, Bayes and VI with
  exact gradients, and simulation draws each distribution. `transform KIND(SHAPE)` is the
  omega-line spelling of `[eta_shape]`.
