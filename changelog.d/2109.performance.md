- **A profiled θ level block's Newton step takes its gradient from the adjoint** (#2109):
  on the analytic gradient route, each subject's gradient over its levels is one
  adjoint recording instead of two EBE re-solves per level. The same objective is
  minimised; the solve, and the covariance step's re-profiling, run faster.
  `gradient = fd` keeps the differences, and a subject the adjoint declines takes
  them alone, reported as `W_PROFILE_FD_GRADIENT`.
