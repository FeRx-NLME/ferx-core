- **Models with an `[event_model]` or `[binary_model]` endpoint now get a correct
  per-subject score** (#1744). The score feeds `covariance_method = s` / `rsr` and
  the gradient-based outer optimizers, and it covered only the Gaussian
  observations. Under `method = focei`, a TTE-only or binary-only model scored zero
  for every subject. `covariance_method = s` then failed as singular, and `rsr`
  reported SE = 0 with no warning. The fit stayed at the initial estimates under
  `optimizer = lbfgs`, `slsqp` and `trust_region`, under `method = gn`, and under
  `method = gn_hybrid` with a non-BOBYQA polish. Each such fit carried
  `W_STALLED_AT_INIT`, but all except `trust_region` also reported convergence. A
  joint PK + TTE model lost the hazard term from its score under both `focei` and
  `foce`. Fits that used `optimizer = auto` (which picks BOBYQA for these models)
  and the default `covariance_method = r` were unaffected. On a TTE library model,
  the RSR SEs now agree with NONMEM's to within 0.14%.
