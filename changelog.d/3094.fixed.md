- **A reset on a subject's first record no longer blanks `[derived]` compartments or
  warns** (#3094). That reset includes an `EVID=4` first dose, and it changes nothing. On
  an analytical model, such a subject now gets finite `compartments[i]` columns and
  integrals, and `W_DERIVED_CMT_RESET_ANALYTICAL` is not raised for it. Under
  `[diffusion]` it no longer raises `W_SDE_RESET`. An IOV subject with such a reset now
  multi-starts the way one without it does, as non-IOV subjects have since #2995.
