- **A parameter that moves an `[odes]` `if` switch is no longer silently frozen** (#2649):
  an `if` that switches during the integration at a time a parameter can move, such
  as `if (tau > LAGT)` or `if (central > 20)`, puts a jump in the sensitivities that
  exact derivatives cannot see. A θ reaching the equations only that way got an
  analytic gradient of exactly zero and stayed at its initial value, sometimes with
  `converged: true`. These models now run on finite-difference gradients, with a
  warning naming a parameter, so a fit of one may take longer than it did. `min`,
  `max`, `clamp` with literal bounds, covariate-only thresholds and clocks compared
  against literals stay analytic.
