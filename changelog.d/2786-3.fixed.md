- The SDE/EKF likelihood no longer finite-differences the state Jacobian: the filter is written once over a number type (`T = f64` for the value, the recording type for plans) and takes the exact forward tangent of the compiled `[odes]` right-hand side, with `p_obs` within 1e-10 (relative) of the finite-difference path it replaces. SDE models now take the exact adjoint gradient too (FOCE/FOCEI outer and inner gradient, exact inner Hessian): the EKF's process-noise variance is recorded as extra kernel rows with σ as plan inputs (#2786). AGQ/Laplace (the node predictions carry the variance rows), the covariance R-matrix, the held-EBE and fixed-η scores, IOV, `[scaling]`/LTBS and time-varying covariates are exact on an SDE model too; an SS dose, a modeled `RATE`/`D` dose and a rate-defined infusion under an estimated `F` are exact too: the filter's moving window end carries its derivatives through the mean solve's span and the covariance quadrature's sub-steps.
