- **A finite-difference gradient probe the EBE guard rejects is no longer differenced as a
  value** (#2378). The reconverged FD outer gradient and the reported final gradient
  scored such a probe at the objective's `1e20` wall, which is finite, so one component of
  the gradient came out near `1e23` (measured on an ODE + IOV model whose inner solves hit
  `inner_maxiter`). A rejected probe now counts as missing: that component takes the
  one-sided difference from the other side, or is zero when both sides are rejected.
