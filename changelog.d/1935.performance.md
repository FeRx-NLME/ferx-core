- **The finite-difference covariance step no longer runs its setup on one thread** (#1935).
  The base reconvergence at the estimate and the step-size probe now reconverge in parallel,
  and so does the subset base point on the per-subject salvage route. The probe's points are
  also no longer evaluated twice: they are the stencil's own diagonal points, so the stencil
  now takes them from the probe. Without step halving, the step costs `2·n_free²` population
  objectives instead of `2·n_free + 2·n_free²`. On `examples/three_cpt_iv_ode.ferx` with
  `analytic_cov_hessian = false` at 8 threads, the time outside the stencil fell from
  2.0–2.4 s to 0.4–0.5 s. The covariance matrix is bit-identical to before, at every thread
  count. With `verbose = true` the probe now prints `step probe k/N` progress lines, so the
  wait before the first `Hessian k/N` line no longer looks like a hang.
