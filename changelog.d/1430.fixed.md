- **The docs now say what `maxiter` counts** (#1430). On the NLopt optimizers it caps
  objective evaluations, `maxiter × (n + 1)` for `n` packed parameters (plus `40 × (n + 1)` for
  `bobyqa`), and a fit's `n_iterations` counts those evaluations; on `trust_region` and
  Gauss–Newton it caps iterations. The fit-options page, the FAQ, the CLI page and the
  `FitOptions::outer_maxiter` rustdoc said "iterations" for every optimizer.
