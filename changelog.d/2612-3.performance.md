- **Faster SAEM M-steps** (#2612): the score-based M-step skips the theta score when every
  theta has a closed-form update, takes the information's prediction derivatives in one
  forward pass, and reuses each subject's cached event schedule; the covariate
  mu-reference update takes an exact Jacobian of its compiled typical value. With the new
  kernels, SAEM runs 1.1–1.7× faster on the benchmarks with numerically estimated
  parameters.
