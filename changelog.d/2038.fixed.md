- **`W_NONFINITE_OBJECTIVE` names the subjects behind it, and no longer calls a
  finite objective the divergence sentinel** (#2038): when a subject's own
  objective contribution is unusable, the warning ends by naming it, with the
  contribution and the `TIME` of its first observation whose `IPRED` or `PRED` is
  not finite. The warning's `details` gain a `subjects` array listing each one. An
  objective past the `1e14` cutoff but below the `1e20` clamp is now reported as
  "too large to be a fit" rather than "at the divergence sentinel", since no
  subject was clamped.
