- **`[odes]` subjects with a moving event time keep exact derivatives only up to second order**
  (#486). This covers an estimated `LAGTIME`, a per-route `lag=` and a modeled duration `D{n}`.
  The adjoint's event walk is exact through second order there, which serves the FOCE/FOCEI
  gradient, the inner Hessian and the Gauss–Newton AGQ gradient. The third-order quantities
  were wrong by up to 3e-1 relative: the analytic R-matrix and the Laplace exact-anchor
  gradient. Those subjects now decline. The covariance step salvages them numerically
  (`W_COV_ANALYTIC_SALVAGE`), and Laplace scores them by reconverged finite differences.
