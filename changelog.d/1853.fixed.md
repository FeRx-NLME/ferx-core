- **A `TIME`-dependent disposition on an ODE-accumulated (drug-driven) hazard is now
  refused instead of silently frozen** (#1853): the hazard pass integrates one `$PK`
  snapshot taken at `TIME = 0`, while the Gaussian half of the same objective reads
  `[individual_parameters]` at every record, so the two halves integrated different
  concentration curves. Measured on `CL = TVCL · (1 + 0.05 · TIME)` with a drug-driven
  hazard: `central(20)` read as 13.53 against the predictions' 3.07, `H(20)` 10.1 % high,
  and the hazard term bit-identical whether the `TIME` coefficient was `0` or `0.05`.
  `fit()`, `predict()`, `predict_survival()` and `simulate()` now return an error naming
  the parameter and whether the `[odes]` RHS or an `init(...)` seed reads it — the `TIME`
  axis of the freeze #741 refuses on the covariate axis. A `TIME`-reading **dose-record**
  quantity (`MTT`, `ALAG1`, `F`, `D{n}`/`R{n}`, …) is read at its own dose record (#1575)
  and still fits, as does a `TIME` read that stays inside the `[odes]` RHS, which the
  integrator clock resolves at every step.
