- **The outer-gradient fallback warning now says why each subject fell back** (#2713):
  instead of "a shape no analytic recorder serves, or a trial point where it could not
  be formed", it counts the declined subjects by cause — a data shape no recorder
  serves, an inner Hessian that was singular or indefinite at an EBE off a strict mode
  (the signature of multimodal EBEs, whose mode changes can make the objective jump
  under the line search), or a recording that failed or went non-finite at a trial
  point. A large FOCEI fallback share can now be traced to one of these.
