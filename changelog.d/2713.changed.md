- **The outer-gradient fallback warning now says why subjects fell back, and whether
  they did at the final estimates** (#2713). The old catch-all ("a shape no analytic
  recorder serves, or a trial point where it could not be formed") is replaced by a
  count of the declined subjects per cause: a data shape no recorder serves, an inner
  Hessian that was singular or indefinite at an EBE off a strict mode, or a recording
  that failed or went non-finite at a trial point. A new sentence counts the subjects
  the gradient still salvaged at the best point the optimizer reached, so a fit that
  ends in `Failure` shows whether the fixed-EBE fallback affected its final gradient.
  A subject is now treated as a structural (shape) decline only when its recording fails
  both at the trial's EBE and at `η = 0`. A recording that fails only at an extreme
  trial-point EBE now takes the held-EBE gradient instead of the reconverged one.
