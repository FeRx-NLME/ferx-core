- **The outer-gradient fallback warning now says why subjects fell back, and whether
  they still do at the reported estimates** (#2713). The old catch-all ("a shape no
  analytic recorder serves, or a trial point where it could not be formed") is replaced
  by a count of the declined subjects per cause: a data shape no recorder serves, an
  inner Hessian that was singular or indefinite at an EBE off a strict mode, or a
  recording that failed at a trial point. After the optimizer returns, the subjects the
  exact gradient still declines at the reported estimates are counted the same way, so
  a fit that ends in `Failure` shows whether its final gradient carries the fixed-EBE
  approximation.
  A declined subject now takes the always-reconverged "structural" salvage only when the
  decline is its data shape (including an output scale no recorder serves, such as a
  per-compartment `obs_scale[CMT=n]` on an ODE model). A recording that fails at some parameter values only, such
  as a lagged dose arrival landing on a covariate-change row, takes the held-EBE gradient
  on the `reconverge_gradient_interval` schedule, like any other trial-point decline.
