- **A fit warm-started at a converged optimum now reports converged** (#2506): a
  bootstrap sample or refit started at a previous fit's estimates does not move, and
  was reported `converged = false` with `W_STALLED_AT_INIT`. When the outer optimizer
  (NLopt, the built-in BFGS loops, `trust_region` or Gauss–Newton) does not leave its
  start it now restarts the optimizer from a perturbation of that
  start. If the restart converges back to the same objective (within 0.1), the start
  was an optimum: the fit is reported converged, is not flagged, passes
  `Strictness::default()`, and records it in the new `FitResult::stationary_start`. If
  the restart finds a lower objective, the start was a stall and the restart's result
  is reported instead, with a warning saying so. Any model and objective is covered,
  AGQ and mixtures included; the cost is one extra optimization, paid only by a fit
  that did not move.
