- **A dense-BFGS inner solve with no preconditioner no longer takes an unscaled
  steepest-descent step after a non-descent BFGS direction** (#2099). This applies to the
  IOV inner solve, which runs on the identity metric. From a steep start that step was
  `‖g‖` long and could leave the line search with no acceptable trial, which sent the
  subject to the Nelder–Mead fallback. The step is now scaled by `1/‖g‖`, the same as the
  solve's first step.
