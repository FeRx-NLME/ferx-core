- **The FOCEI objective of a non-Gaussian model with η is smooth in θ** (#2437): the
  finite-difference η-Hessian in the `½ log|H̃|` term of TTE, binary, categorical and
  CTMM data terms now uses one fixed step (`1e-3`) instead of a Shi (2021) ε^⅓ step
  (~5e-6) re-derived from the data-term gradient at every θ. At the old scale the
  second difference was mostly roundoff (~1e-5 error against closed-form Hessians, now
  ≤ 3.3e-7). That noise made the objective rough in θ. On
  `examples/ctmm_pd_2state.ferx` the BOBYQA optimum moves from OFV 711.679 to 710.945.
  OFVs of these models shift slightly.
