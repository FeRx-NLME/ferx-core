- **The FOCEI objective of a non-Gaussian model with η is smooth in θ** (#2437): the
  finite-difference η-Hessian in the `½ log|H̃|` term of TTE, binary, categorical and
  CTMM data terms now steps `2e-3 · √ω_jj` on each η axis instead of a Shi (2021) ε^⅓
  step (~5e-6) re-derived from the data-term gradient at every θ. At the old scale the
  second difference was mostly roundoff (~1e-5 error against closed-form Hessians, now
  ≤ 3.3e-7). That noise made the objective rough in θ. Scaling by the prior standard
  deviation keeps the objective independent of η's units (`K * ETA` with `ω/K²`).
  OFVs of these models shift slightly. On a flat direction, such as the CL/V ridge of
  `examples/ctmm_pd_2state.ferx`, the point where an optimizer stops can move further.
