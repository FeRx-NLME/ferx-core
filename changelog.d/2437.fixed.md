- **The FOCEI objective of a non-Gaussian model with η is smooth in θ** (#2437): the
  finite-difference η-Hessian in the `½ log|H̃|` term of TTE, binary, categorical and
  CTMM data terms now steps `2.4e-3` diagonal posterior SDs, `1/√(1/ω_jj + Ĥ_jj)`, on
  each η axis instead of a Shi (2021) ε^⅓ step (~5e-6) re-derived from the data-term
  gradient at every θ (`0.1` posterior SDs for a time-inhomogeneous CTMM, whose adaptive
  occupancy integration is the limit there). At the old scale the
  second difference was mostly roundoff (~1e-5 error against closed-form Hessians, now
  ≤ 2.4e-6 at every supported ω). That noise made the objective rough in θ. Scaling by
  the posterior standard deviation keeps the objective independent of η's units
  (`K * ETA` with `ω/K²`).
  OFVs of these models shift slightly. On a flat direction the point where an optimizer
  stops can move further: on `examples/ctmm_pd_2state.ferx`, whose LQ01 / CL / V ridge
  runs to the LQ01 bound, BOBYQA now reaches OFV 709.07 (711.68 before) and L-BFGS 709.40.
