- **The FOCEI objective of a non-Gaussian model with η is smooth in θ** (#2437): the
  η-Hessian in the `½ log|H̃|` term of an analytic-family TTE (exponential, Weibull,
  Gompertz; single or repeated events), binary and time-homogeneous CTMM data term is now
  exact, from second-order dual numbers through the endpoint's expressions, instead of a
  finite difference on a Shi (2021) ε^⅓ step (~5e-6) re-derived from the data-term
  gradient at every θ. At that scale the second difference was mostly roundoff (~1e-5
  against closed-form Hessians), which made the objective rough in θ. The exact
  Hessians agree with independent references to 3e-10, and the objective no longer
  depends on η's units (`K * ETA` with `ω/K²`, equal to 9e-16). Endpoints the duals do
  not reach (a drug-driven hazard, a time-inhomogeneous CTMM, more than 8 η) keep a
  finite difference, on a step of `2.4e-3` conditional posterior SDs (`0.1` for a
  time-inhomogeneous CTMM, whose adaptive occupancy integration is the limit there).
  OFVs of these models shift slightly. On a flat direction the point where an optimizer
  stops can move further: on `examples/ctmm_pd_2state.ferx`, whose LQ01 / CL / V ridge
  runs to the LQ01 bound, BOBYQA now reaches OFV 709.07 (711.68 before) and L-BFGS 709.40.
