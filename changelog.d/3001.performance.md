- **The numerical θ/σ M-step of SAEM, IMP and IMPMAP predicts once per solve** (#3001): the
  M-step's η/κ (SAEM) or importance samples (IMP, IMPMAP) are fixed within a solve and
  predictions never depend on σ, so every evaluation at an unchanged θ — all of them in a
  σ-only M-step — now reuses one prediction pass per subject (per sample) instead of
  predicting again. Outputs are bit-identical (fit file and sdtab byte-for-byte, 90 fits);
  whole SAEM fits take 1.3–2.0× fewer instructions on the benchmarks with a numerical M-step
  (1.33× `pk1`, 1.96× `pk1_ode`, 1.31× `pk1_sparse`, 1.42× `pk2_big`, 1.37× `wiov`).
