- **A SAEM fit now reports the importance-sampled −2 log L as its OFV** (#2973), the
  NONMEM `$EST METHOD=SAEM` → `$EST METHOD=IMP EONLY=1` convention, with its Monte Carlo
  standard error; AIC and BIC are built from it. The FOCE/Laplace objective at the same
  estimates is kept as a separately labelled diagnostic (`saem_objective.foce_ofv`,
  `ofv_foce_laplace` in `fit.yaml`, a `FOCE/Laplace OFV (diagnostic)` line in the report).
  New option `saem_ofv = imp | agq | focei` (default `imp`; `agq` is deterministic adaptive
  quadrature, `focei` restores the previous value) with `saem_ofv_samples` (3000) and
  `saem_ofv_nodes` (11), and `saem_ofv_pilot_samples` (300: draws per proposal-fitting pilot
  pass, which keeps the readout under 2× a SAEM fit on the bench models). The estimates are unchanged; SAEM OFVs from earlier versions are
  FOCE/Laplace values and are not comparable with the new default.
