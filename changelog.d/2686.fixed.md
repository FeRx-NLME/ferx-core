- IMP and IMPMAP fits no longer report `converged: true` when the run stopped at its iteration
  limit without finishing. `fit()` now reads the floor of the trailing objective and demotes the
  fit with `W_MCEM_NOT_SETTLED` if that floor was still falling, or stayed well above the final
  marginal. A run of fewer than 30 iterations is too short to test, keeps its verdict and gets
  `W_MCEM_SETTLING_UNTESTED` (#2686).
