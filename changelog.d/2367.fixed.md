- **An `ADDL`-implied dose now takes a route's own lag from the row that carried `ADDL`**
  (#2367), as it already did for the compartment `lagtime`. This covers a route written
  `first_order(ka=…, lag=L)` and every other lagged absorption kind. ferx used to read the
  route lag at the implied dose's governing record. When a covariate in `L` changed along the
  train, the implied dose then arrived at the wrong time. A first-order route lagged by `L` (with constant `KA`) is
  a depot dosed with `ALAG = L`, and NONMEM fixes that `ALAG` at the parent row.
