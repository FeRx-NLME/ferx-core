- `method = gn` no longer reports `converged: true` when its trust radius has collapsed: a small
  OFV change on a step capped by a radius below 1e-3 is not taken as convergence unless the step
  is interior to the region, so the run now ends on the collapse or the iteration budget with
  `converged: false` (previously e.g. `warfarin_bloq` stopped 10.6 OFV above FOCEI as
  converged). A collapsed or cancelled run also no longer carries the contradictory
  "max iterations reached without convergence" warning; the collapse warnings now read
  "… trust radius collapsed without convergence" and are classified critical/convergence
  (#2738, #1049).
