  flip-flop regime is now reported** (#1823): the up-front refusal samples η = 0
  typical values and the `W_UNCERTAINTY_DRAWS_SKIPPED` skip samples each draw's
  η = 0 θ, so an individual whose *sampled* η pushed `ke` past the tilting
  abscissa was predicted as an identically-zero profile (`ipred = 0`, and under
  a proportional error model `DV = 0`) with nothing said on any channel —
  measured at 5.9% of rows on a 1-cpt transit form at `ke = 0.125`, `KTR = 0.2`,
  ω²~CL~ = 0.09. `simulate_with_options_diag()` and
  `simulate_with_uncertainty_diag()` now carry one per-run
  `W_SIMULATED_FLIP_FLOP` count (typed `flip_flop`) naming the family and the
  ODE `transit()` / `igd()` rewrite, which reroutes per individual. A
  twin-carrying model is unaffected: it already reroutes to its ODE twin.
