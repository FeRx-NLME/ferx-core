- **A θ with a negative lower bound and a large initial value is estimated again**
  (#2146): such a θ is optimised on its natural scale, and when the model has one
  the outer optimizer used no parameter scaling at all. A θ started at, say,
  1e6 then stayed at its initial value while the fit reported convergence. Each
  identity-packed θ whose initial value exceeds 1e3 in magnitude is now divided by
  that magnitude under the default `parameter_scaling = auto` (and `abs`), with
  every optimizer except `trust_region`. On warfarin with `V = TVV_S / 1e5` and
  `TVV_S` started at 1e6, L-BFGS, SLSQP and BOBYQA reached OFV −265.42 before and
  now reach the minimum, −286.00. An explicit
  `parameter_scaling = none` is unchanged.
