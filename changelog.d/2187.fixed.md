- **The outer, mixture, SAEM and VI finite differences no longer step a small θ by an
  absolute amount** (#2187). After #2147 fixed the covariance step, the reconverged FD
  outer gradient still stepped a packed coordinate by `1e-4·(1 + |x|)`, which on an
  identity-packed θ (negative lower bound) is about `1e-4` in the θ's own units: on a θ of
  8e-4 its gradient was 15 % off, and a θ below 1e-4 was stepped through zero into the
  bound penalty. That gradient serves `reconverge_gradient_interval`, IOV models under
  `gradient = fd` or outside the analytic IOV scope, the per-subject salvage of the mixed
  analytic gradient, and the reported `final_gradient` of a derivative-free fit. Such a θ,
  when its declared θ₀ is non-zero and below 1 in magnitude, is now stepped by
  `1e-4·(|θ₀| + |x|)` there, and by `1e-5·|θ₀|` in a mixture model's FD outer gradient
  (previously a flat `1e-5`). The SAEM M-step gradient and Fisher information and the VI
  ELBO gradient take forward differences in θ itself, so there any θ with
  `0 < |θ₀| < 1`, log- or identity-packed, is now stepped by `1e-5·(|θ₀| + |θ|)` instead
  of `1e-5·(1 + |θ|)`, which was a first-order error of about 1 % on a θ of 1e-3. Every
  other coordinate's step is unchanged bit for bit.
