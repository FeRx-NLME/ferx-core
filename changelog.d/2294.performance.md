- **Faster exact gradients for models with many θ** (#2294). The adjoint gradient
  no longer copies every θ's name and bounds once per subject per evaluation. On a
  1,317-θ covariate network that copying was about a third of the gradient: one
  population gradient took ~10 ms → ~7.6 ms, and ~4.1 ms → ~3.25 ms at 405 θ.
  Gradients are unchanged, and models with few θ are unaffected.
