- **Discrete-time Markov model endpoint, `[markov_model] type = dtmm`** (#2473): each
  `transition A -> B = <expr>` is the probability that the state observed at a record is
  `B` given that the previous observed state was `A` (the NONMEM `PREVIOUS`-DV `$PRED`
  model). The stay probability is filled in, the first record is conditioned on, a step is
  a record whatever its spacing, and `TIME` reads the scored record's time. Fitted by
  `focei`, `saem` and `imp`, with an exact analytic inner gradient; matches NONMEM
  `LAPLACE LIKE` on a 2-state and a 3-state cumulative-logit model. Time-varying
  covariates, drug-driven probabilities and simulation are not supported yet and are
  refused.
