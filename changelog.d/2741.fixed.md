- **Adaptive dosing now refuses a residual-error magnitude instead of ignoring it** (#2741):
  `simulate_adaptive` and `simulate_adaptive_from_spec` drew the controller's `Dv` reading
  from the bare sigma, so a magnitude expression (`proportional(PROP * WT)`), a `weight =`
  or a `power(...)` exponent on a single-endpoint `[error_model]` was silently dropped from
  the signal while the run reported success. These now fail with `E_ADAPTIVE_UNSUPPORTED`,
  as a magnitude on a `CMT=N:` line already did. Plain `simulate()` is unchanged and applies
  the magnitude.
