- **NONMEM's SAEM independence kernels** (#2612): `isample_m1` proposes each subject's
  random effects from the population distribution and `isample_m1b` from the subject's own
  accumulated conditional mean and covariance, before the random-walk moves, as NONMEM's
  `ISAMPLE_M1` / `ISAMPLE_M1B` do (default 2 each; under IOV the η block). They run where the
  M-step is SAEM's own; a model on the derivative-free fallback (a mixture, M3, TTE) keeps
  the random walk, since better mixing raises that rule's bias.
