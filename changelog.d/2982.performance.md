- **Mu-referenced models take a cheaper covariance step** (#2982): a θ that reaches the
  likelihood only through mu-referenced lines (`TVCL * exp(ETA_CL)`, covariate powers, logit
  and additive forms, each with an η no other line reads) rides on the random effects' lanes,
  so on a model with fewer than four random effects the covariance step's prediction passes
  run one lane per random effect. Standard errors are unchanged (to rounding).
