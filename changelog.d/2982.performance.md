- **Mu-referenced models take a cheaper covariance step and FOCE/FOCEI gradient** (#2982):
  a θ that reaches the likelihood only through mu-referenced lines (`TVCL * exp(ETA_CL)`,
  covariate powers, logit and additive forms, each with an η no other line reads) rides on
  the random effects' lanes, so the covariance step's prediction passes run one lane per
  random effect, and a fully mu-referenced FOCE/FOCEI subject's gradient needs no reverse
  sweep through its predictions. Estimates and standard errors are unchanged (to rounding).
