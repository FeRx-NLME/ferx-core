- **A fit with a `profile`d θ level block steps its covariance stencil at `3e-3` by
  default** (#2414), not the global `fd_hessian_step = 1e-2`. The profile objective's FD
  Hessian is about ten times further from the exact one at the same step. At `1e-2` a
  profiled fit's standard errors sat 0.4–0.75% (`r`) and up to 4% (`rsr`) away from the
  unprofiled fit's exact ones; at `3e-3` they are within 0.08%. An explicit
  `fd_hessian_step` is used as written, `1e-2` included. A caller building `FitOptions` by
  hand raises the new `fd_hessian_step_set` with the step.
