- **SAEM and Bayes η proposals that are certain to be rejected skip the prediction**
  (#2608): the Metropolis–Hastings uniform is now drawn before the proposal is
  scored, and a proposal whose prior plus a proven lower bound on its data term
  already fails the acceptance test is rejected without running the model. Every
  accept/reject decision is unchanged, so fits are bit-identical at a fixed seed;
  measured CPU savings are 2–3 % on sparse analytic and event-driven benches and
  under 1 % on ODE benches. Not yet applied to IOV models' η and κ steps.
