- **Reconverged finite-difference gradients re-solve the EBEs at an inner tolerance of at most
  `1e-7`** (#2261): the FD outer gradients (used for IOV models without the analytic gradient,
  and by `reconverge_gradient_interval`), the per-subject salvage gradients, the reported
  final gradient and the AGQ finite-difference score start each probe at the mode, and at the
  default `inner_tol = 1e-5` such a probe could stop almost where it began, losing part of
  the EBE's response from the difference (up to 9e-4 relative error in a gradient, measured).
  These gradients can move in their trailing digits. The covariance step keeps
  `cov_inner_tol`.
