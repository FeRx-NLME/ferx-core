- **An `ode_method = auto` fit no longer aborts out of memory in the post-fit tolerance check**
  (#3161): when the check's tighter `ode_reltol` named another stiff stepper than the fit's
  (`1e-8` against `1e-9`), the replay of an ODE segment's recording met another structure, and
  the segment's whole adaptive loop was then traced into that discarded recording, up to
  `ode_max_steps` steps. A recording that fails now returns at once, and the subject is
  recorded afresh, as it was before.
