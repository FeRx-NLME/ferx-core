- **A dose implied by `ADDL` now runs under the covariates of the record that ends its
  interval, as in NONMEM** (#1909). NONMEM does not evaluate `$PK` at an `ADDL`-implied dose:
  the interval across it uses the next record at or after the dose. ferx gave every implied
  dose the covariates of the row that carried `ADDL`, so on a subject whose time-varying
  covariates changed after that row, the parent row's values governed up to each dose of the
  train. Predictions after the change were 10–30 % off in the anchor. Constant-covariate
  subjects, and trains whose covariates do not change, are unaffected.
