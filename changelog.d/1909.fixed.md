- **A dose implied by `ADDL` now takes its covariates, `F`, `TIME` and occasion from the
  record that ends its interval, and its lag from the row that carried `ADDL`, as in
  NONMEM** (#1909). NONMEM does not evaluate `$PK` at an `ADDL`-implied dose: the interval
  across it uses the next record at or after the dose, in (`TIME`, file order), while the
  dose's `ALAG` is the one set on the parent row. ferx gave every implied dose the parent
  row's values. On a subject whose time-varying covariates changed after that row, the
  parent row's covariates and bioavailability governed every dose of the train, and a
  `TIME`-dependent `F` read the parent's time. Predictions after the change were off by up to
  30 % in the anchors. Constant-covariate subjects, and trains whose covariates do not
  change, are unaffected.
