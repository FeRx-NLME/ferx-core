- Adaptive dosing: the outcome metrics `signal_min`, `signal_max`, `signal_mean` and
  `pct_time_in_window` are now scored on the latent (noise-free) monitor value, like
  `auc_target_attainment`, instead of the assay-noised reading. Results change for `Dv`
  (`with_assay_error`) monitors only; `Ipred` monitors are unaffected. Each
  `ObservedSignal` now records `latent` next to `value` (which stays the reading the
  controller saw), and the struct is `#[non_exhaustive]` (#946).
