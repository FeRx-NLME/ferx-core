- **SIR no longer scores a draw at a rejected inner start** (#2382): on an ODE model
  with IOV, a draw whose warm EBE re-solve rejected a subject at its inner start was
  weighted from that subject's unoptimised start with a zero H-matrix. Such a draw now
  gets weight 0, a `SIR:` warning says how many draws did, and the all-draws-rejected
  error counts them. A nested-level IOV warm start that cannot be compacted now keeps
  η̂ rather than starting the subject cold (unreachable from a parsed model).
