- `ode_method = auto` no longer demotes a segment from a stiff stepper back to RK45 on a single
  non-stiff periodic probe reading. The mid-segment switch is now one-way (explicit to stiff), which
  removes an objective error of up to 0.14 OFV and the jitter along a parameter scan on stiff-ish
  target-mediated-disposition models; fits that never demoted are bit-identical (#2845).
