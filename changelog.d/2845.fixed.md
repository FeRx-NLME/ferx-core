- `ode_method = auto` no longer demotes a segment from a stiff stepper back to RK45 on a single
  non-stiff periodic probe reading. The mid-segment switch is now one-way (explicit to stiff). On
  stiff-ish target-mediated-disposition models this removes an objective error of up to 0.4 OFV at
  default tolerances, and up to 31 OFV under `gradient = fd` at `ode_reltol = 1e-6`, together with
  most of the jitter along a parameter scan; fits that never demoted are bit-identical (#2845).
