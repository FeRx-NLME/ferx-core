- **An analytical model's `[initial_conditions]` baseline is laid down again at an `EVID=3/4` reset** (#3140), as NONMEM re-applies `A_0` and `[odes] init(...)` already re-seeds. Before, the reset dropped it from every later observation, so an `EVID=4` first dose (the usual NONMEM layout) lost the whole baseline: 75% of the prediction on a one-compartment probe. The amount is still the subject's `t = 0` evaluation, where NONMEM re-evaluates it at the reset row: a baseline that reads a covariate, or an individual parameter that reads `TIME`, and steps at the reset needs an `[odes]` model.
