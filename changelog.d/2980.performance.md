- **The exact covariance R-matrix is cheaper on every Laplacian method** (#2980): its
  Hessian columns come from one forward-over-reverse sweep per pass of two directions instead
  of one reverse per direction. Across 22 covariance benchmarks (FOCE, FOCEI, Laplace,
  FOCEI-AGQ and Laplace-AGQ; closed-form and ODE models) the covariance step runs 0.41–0.80×
  the instructions it took before (0.60× in total) and whole fits 0.70–0.98×; no benchmark is
  slower. The same sweep serves the covariate-NN Hessian columns and the exact Hessians of
  small recorded functions. Standard errors are unchanged (to rounding).
