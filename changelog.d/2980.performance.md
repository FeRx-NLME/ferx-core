- **The exact covariance R-matrix is cheaper, most on models with many parameters** (#2980):
  its Hessian columns come from one forward-over-reverse sweep per pass of two directions
  instead of one reverse per direction. Across 22 covariance benchmarks (FOCE, FOCEI, Laplace,
  FOCEI-AGQ and Laplace-AGQ; closed-form and ODE models) the covariance step takes 0.78× the
  instructions in total (0.72× on the two-compartment AGQ fits), and whole fits 0.95×. The
  smallest one-compartment FOCEI/Laplace fits pay up to 2% more. The same sweep serves the
  covariate-NN Hessian columns and the exact Hessians of small recorded functions. Standard
  errors are unchanged (to rounding).
